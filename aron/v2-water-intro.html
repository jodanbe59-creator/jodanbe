About

ARON Design is an interior design studio for clinics, hospitals and commercial spaces. We shape every project around the people who will use it — the patient who walks in a little nervous, and the doctor who works there every day.

Soft curves, layered light and honest, lasting materials are how we make a space feel calm, whatever its palette. From the first idea to the final detail, we design every part of it with care.
