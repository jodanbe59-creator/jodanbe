Materials

Chosen at the source, not from a catalogue.

We visit the quarry, the forest and the foundry before a drawing is final. Each travertine slab is book-matched by hand, and every bronze profile is cast for one house only.

Craftsmanship

Engineering

Eight metres of floor over open air.

The upper slab is post-tensioned and tied back into the rock, so the living room reaches past the cliff edge with no column below it. Our engineers stay on site from the first core sample to the last pour.
